Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the information needed to provide a personal expense tracking experience. When you create an account and use the app, this may include:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and encrypted password credentials.
  • Expense records: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, and optional descriptions you log.
  • Basic technical data: Standard session and request details required to keep you signed in and secure the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to operate and maintain Spendly for your personal use, including:

  • Authenticating your account and securing access to your dashboard.
  • Storing, organizing, filtering, and summarizing your logged expenses.
  • Troubleshooting issues, maintaining reliability, and improving core app features.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense entries are stored in Spendly's application database for as long as your account remains active. We apply reasonable technical safeguards to protect stored records from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

You may review, edit, or delete individual expense entries at any time within the app.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial tracking data to advertisers or data brokers.

To render typography cleanly, pages may load web fonts from Google Fonts, which receives standard browser request metadata (such as your IP address and browser user agent) when fonts are fetched. No expense records or account details are shared with external services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled in Spendly, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
